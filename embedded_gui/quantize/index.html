Low-color quantization, Bayer ordered dithering, and dirty band optimization.

Enables smooth rendering of gradients, images, and anti-aliased geometry onto monochrome OLEDs (1-bit), grayscale e-paper (2-bit / 4-bit), and RGB565 displays without banding.
